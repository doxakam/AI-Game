RANDOM HEROES PVP

10-minute fantasy arena · 2–8 players · no accounts

📖 Read the game rules and controls before playing

Getting started

Enter your nickname, create a room or join with a six-character code. Two to eight players can play. A random hero is assigned to you. The match lasts 10 minutes; last survivor wins.

Movement and combat

Click the arena to capture your mouse. Move mouse to look, WASD to walk, left click to attack, V to change camera, Q/E to use abilities, and Esc to release mouse. Your first weapon is picked up automatically when you walk near it; you can hold only one weapon. Press F to replace it with a nearby weapon.

Warrior shield

Warriors may carry one off-hand shield in addition to their weapon. Walk near a shield and press F to equip it. Hold R to raise the shield and reduce incoming damage by 5% at level 1, up to 20% at level 4. It starts with a 5-second cooldown and has a 5-second cooldown after lowering. You cannot attack while blocking. Upgrade it at a fixed shop.

Survival and shops

All heroes have a visible 30-second spawn protection bubble. Fight monsters for gold and XP, collect gear, and choose buffs on level-up. Shops only work when you stand near their golden markers. Press B to shop, 1 for a health potion, and 2 for mana. The arena shrinks later in the match.

Elimination and surrender

No respawns. If eliminated, choose Spectate to keep watching. You can surrender at any time using the Surrender button; this eliminates your hero but keeps you in the room as a spectator.

Move with WASD or the left joystick. Click the arena to lock mouse look; move mouse to turn, Esc to unlock. Attack with click or FIRE. Use Q/E for abilities, 1/2 for potions. Level up to choose one of three buffs. Last hero standing wins!
